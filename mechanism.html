Measurement conditions

SS = mean last five responses / P1. Recovery = (probe − last-five mean) / (P1 − last-five mean), branching from the full post-conditioning state. P1 absolute units are released vesicles; use a ratio for EPSC amplitude comparisons if quantal size is unchanged.
