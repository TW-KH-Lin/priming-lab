Interpretation and limitations

A higher resting TS occupancy can arise from increasing forward priming or reducing backward unpriming. Similar phenotype reproduction therefore does not necessarily identify the original parameter combination.

Compare both the minimal candidate and alternatives in the app. A solution outside the strict near-optimal error tolerance is labelled explicitly, even when all measured readouts fall within their uncertainty scales. “Insufficient sampling” is not evidence of a precise or unique parameter estimate.

The default minimal criterion uses error close to the best tested solution. The optional 90% coverage criterion is more permissive and may select a single-parameter approximation that misses several individual targets.

The legacy HK preset in the original forward simulator differs from Table S2. It was not used as ground truth here and has not been changed. This benchmark uses the independently transcribed Table S2 values.
